<div class="about-text">
  <p>I'm someone trying to grow in the world of IT and cybersecurity. My journey started with cybersecurity, and over time IT became part of it too.</p>

  <p>Something I noticed while learning: Turkish-language resources are much harder to find than English ones. So I started translating what I learn into Turkish — partly to build my own archive, and partly to help someone else on the same path. That's why this site's primary language is Turkish, though everything is also available here in English.</p>

  <p>I try to write in a way that's professional but still warm — as if I'm explaining it to a friend. Breaking down complex topics into simple language helps reinforce my own learning, and hopefully helps whoever's reading too.</p>
