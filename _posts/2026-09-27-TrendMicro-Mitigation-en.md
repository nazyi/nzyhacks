## Mitigation

Let's look at the **Security Playbooks** page under the Workflow and Automation tab.

On this page you can automate a lot of actions and reduce your workload. You can build an automation from scratch or from a ready-made template, and set whether it should be triggered periodically, manually, or automatically, depending on the automation type. Let's look at an example playbook, "Leaked account."
